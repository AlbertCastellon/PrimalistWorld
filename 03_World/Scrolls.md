# Scrolls

Scrolls are objects created by [[Scholar]]s with spells inscribed on them. Depending on their purpose, they are divided into different types.

---
## Instant Spell-Scrolls
Instant Spell-Scrolls allow a Wielder to cast a spell they have not learned by infusing the scroll with [[Aether]]. The scroll acts as a set of grooves through which the spell is cast and is destroyed in the process.
The number of uniums required to use a scroll depends on the user's level of Aether manipulation. As a result, Wielders with insufficient Aether manipulation or Well Capacity cannot cast particularly powerful spells, even if they possess the corresponding scroll.
### Escape Scrolls
Escape Scrolls are a type of Instant Spell-Scroll inscribed with the Escape spell. They allow Wielders to exit [[Rifts]] without collecting the domain flower, either to preserve the Rift or to avoid confronting [[Arcane Beasts]] that are too powerful to defeat.

---
## Spell-Scrolls
Spell-Scrolls allow Wielders to learn a spell at a specified mastery level.
They are created using a [[Spellshards|Spellshard]], which is consumed in the process. Depending on the outcome, this process may produce a number of scrolls or none at all.
Spell-Scrolls can also be created without a Spellshard if the Scholar knows the method required to do so. This allows them to produce an unlimited number of scrolls, provided the necessary materials are available.
