# Ghoul

## Species Type
Undead

---
## Appearance
The Ghoul is a gaunt, corpse-like humanoid beast with decaying gray skin, an oversized head, bulging bloodshot eyes, and a grotesque maw filled with razor-sharp black teeth. Its skeletal frame, swollen belly, and protruding spine give it the appearance of perpetual starvation. It moves primarily on all fours using its long claws and is usually clad only in a tattered leather loincloth.
