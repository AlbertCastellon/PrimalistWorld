## Appearance
The Oracle Snake is a large serpent covered in scales the color of sun-bleached bone. Its deep violet eyes possess an unnerving, intelligent gaze, while its long forked tongue constantly samples the air. It moves with graceful precision and often coils around elevated perches or its chosen master like a living mantle.
