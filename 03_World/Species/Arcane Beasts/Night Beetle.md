# Night Beetle

## Species Type
Beetle

## Appearance
The Night Beetle is a gigantic beetle-like beast with a dark, metallic carapace and six powerful legs ending in razor-sharp claws. Its head is dominated by giant multifaceted eyes, massive branch-like horns, and crushing mandibles lined with grinding teeth capable of shattering rock.
