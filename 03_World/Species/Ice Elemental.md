# Ice Elemental

## Species Type
Elemental

---
## Appearance
The Ice Elemental is a floating construct of solid, translucent ice that refracts light into pale blue and white hues. A freezing gale constantly swirls beneath its suspended torso, while two detached, jagged arms hover at its sides in perfect unison. Its triangular head bears a pair of hollow eyes that glow with an eerie glacial light.
