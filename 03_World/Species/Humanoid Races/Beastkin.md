# Beastkin

## Appearance
Beastkin are humanoids with animal-like features. They appear to encompass multiple variants, potentially distinguished by the animal traits they possess.
## Classes and Subclasses

Beastfolk are known for their talent as warriors, and the Warrior class is confirmed among them. Although other classes may be available to them, their prevalence has not been established.

Beastfolk do not possess unique subclasses exclusive to their race. However, it is uncommon for them to obtain Common or Uncommon subclasses.
