## Species Type
Undead

## Appaerance
The Skeleton Knight is an undead humanoid standing over six feet tall, its skeletal frame formed from polished white bone and clad in archaic black plate armor. A tattered spectral cape trails from its shoulders, while glowing purple lights burn within its hollow eye sockets. It wields a massive double-edged greatsword infused with teal aether, giving it the appearance of a disciplined undead warrior.
