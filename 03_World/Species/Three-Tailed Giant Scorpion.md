## Appearance
The Three-Tailed Giant Scorpion is a colossal scorpion-like beast whose massive body can rival the size of a small house. Its frame is protected by interlocking, stone-like plates that resemble rugged brown rock, while eight thick legs ending in sharp points support its immense weight with surprising stability. Two enormous claws flank powerful, multi-jointed mandibles, and three long, segmented tails arch over its back, each ending in a deadly dark stinger.
