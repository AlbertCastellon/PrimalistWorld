# Claw of the Primal Tiger

## Description
One of the claws of the Primal Tiger, which has absorbed a tremendous amount of the bloodlust before its death.

---
## Effects

- Increases **Agility by 3–6%**.
- Increases **Strength by 3–6%**.
- Reduces the **cooldown of Rage-type spells by 60%**.
- Increases the **effects of Rage-type spells by 15%**.

- Elysha of Spyre (former)
