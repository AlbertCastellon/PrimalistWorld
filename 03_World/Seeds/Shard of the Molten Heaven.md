# Shard of the Molten Heaven

## Description
A piece of shard taken directly from the hardest part of the Molten Heaven.

---
## Effects

- Increases Strength by 4 to 8.
- Increases Toughness by 3 to 6%.
- Increases Arcane Power by 3 to 6%.
- Reduces the cooldown of Earth spells by 25%.
