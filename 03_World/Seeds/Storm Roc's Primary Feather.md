# Storm Roc's Primary Feather

## Description
The largest feather of the Storm Roc, which absorbed a tremendous amount of the Lightning energy by flying through clouds.

---
## Effects

- Increases **Toughness** by 4 to 8.
- Increases **Agility** by 3 to 6%.
- Increases **Arcane Power** by 2 to 4%.
- Increase the effectiveness of Lightning spells by 12%.
