# Artifacts

Artifacts are objects created by [[Artificer]]s or [[Enchanter]]s that grant Wielders various effects and benefits. These include protective effects, temporary increases to one or more [[Stats]], and storage capabilities that allow objects to be stored beyond the artifact's external dimensions, with their weight reduced and the passage of time slowed while inside.

All artifacts require [[Aether]] to be used, although some, such as Endless Pouches, do not require Aether to maintain their storage function and only consume it when retrieving an object.

---
## Restrictions
Artifacts can only be used by Wielders or [[Arcane Beasts]] whose Tier is equal to or higher than that of the artifact. Attempting to use an artifact while below its required Tier results in death.

---
## Kinds or Artifacts

### Storage
The most common type of storage artifact is the Endless Pouch, created by [[Enchanter]]s using a Domain Flower as its main ingredient. Other types of storage artifacts exist that can hold more specialized objects or resources.
### Single-use Artifacts
Some artifacts can only be used once before being destroyed. Examples include Wands, which function like Instant Spell-[[Scrolls]] by allowing their users to cast spells they have not learned, and bombs.
These artifacts are activated by infusing them with Aether.
### Equipable Artifacts
Equipable Artifacts can be used repeatedly and contain an internal reservoir of Aether that depletes as their effects are activated. This reservoir can be recharged once depleted.
Their effects vary widely, ranging from shields that surround and protect the user to temporary Stat increases, Well replenishment, and the ability to restrict a user's power to allow entry into lower-Tier [[Rifts]].
### Utility Artifacts
Utility Artifacts can be used repeatedly by infusing them with Aether. Examples include Aether Cannons and tools used to measure [[Elemental Affinity]].
