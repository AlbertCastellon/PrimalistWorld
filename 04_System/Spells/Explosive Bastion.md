# Explosive Bastion

## Description
Conjures a fortified barrier of Earth aether around the owner that protects them before erupting in a powerful explosion. While active, the bastion hinders the movement of nearby enemies, making escape considerably more difficult.

---
## Effects

- Summons a protective barrier around the caster for **20 seconds**. 
- Reduces the **Agility** of enemies within a **20-foot radius** by **25%** while the barrier is active. 
- The barrier automatically explodes when its duration ends. 
- Barrier durability scales with **Arcane Power**, **Toughness**, **Earth Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Explosion damage scales with **Toughness**, **Earth Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **100 uniums (Iron)** or **180 uniums (Silver)**. 
- **Cooldown:** 5 minutes.

---
## Restrictions

- [[Guardian]]. 
- **120 Toughness**. 
- **80 Arcane Power**. 
- **Earth Affinity:** Peak-Iron or higher.

---
## Known Users

- [[Colossus]]

---
## Notes

- The Agility reduction makes it significantly harder for enemies to escape the explosion's blast radius. - Combines defensive protection, crowd control, and area damage into a single spell. - Evolves from **Explosive Rampart**, enhancing both its battlefield control and offensive potential.
