# Untitled

## Description
Uses Darkness aether to veil the caster's presence behind powerful illusions, concealing their visual appearance and masking their aura. Rather than rendering the user truly invisible, the spell deceives both sight and perception, making them exceedingly difficult to detect.

---

## Effects

- Conceals the caster's presence through illusion-based camouflage. 
- Effectiveness scales with **Darkness Affinity** and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **1 unium per minute**. 
- **Cooldown:** None.

---
## Known Users

- [[Nightmare]]

---
## Notes

- The spell relies on illusions rather than true invisibility. 
- Conceals both the caster's appearance and aether presence, making it effective against visual observation as well as many detection abilities. 
- Detection-oriented spells, highly refined senses, or sufficiently powerful perception abilities may partially or completely overcome the illusion.
