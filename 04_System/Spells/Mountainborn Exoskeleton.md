# Mountainborn Exoskeleton

## Description
Allows the owner to retreat inside their massive natural exoskeleton, transforming it into an impregnable defensive fortress that greatly enhances their resilience.

---
## Effects

- Protects the owner while they remain inside their exoskeleton. 
- Increases **Toughness by 40 + 15%**. 
- Defensive strength scales with **Earth Affinity** and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **0 uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** None.


---
## Restrictions

- Requires a natural exoskeleton capable of housing the owner.

---
## Known Users

- [[Colossus]]

---
## Notes

- The spell can only be used by creatures capable of withdrawing into their exoskeleton. 
- The defensive bonus remains active only while the owner stays inside the shell. 
- Provides one of the highest defensive boosts among Earth-affinity defensive spells, at the cost of greatly limiting mobility.
