# Predator Hunt

## Description
Marks a chosen target with a resonant howl that allows the caster to track its location over great distances while instinctively exploiting its weaknesses, increasing damage dealt against the marked prey.

---
## Effects
- Marks a single target with a resonant howl. 
- Allows the caster to precisely track the marked target over long distances. 
- Increases damage dealt by the caster against the marked target by **15%** for **5 minutes**. 
- Damage bonus scales with **Arcane Power**, **Darkness Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Tracking range increases with **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **60 uniums**. - **Cooldown:** 20 minutes.

## Notes

- Only affects the designated target. 
- The tracking effect persists regardless of the distance separating the caster and the marked target, provided it remains within the spell's maximum range. 
- Primarily used to pursue fleeing prey or ensure that dangerous targets cannot easily escape.
