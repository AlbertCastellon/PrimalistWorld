# Shadow Step

## Description
Allows the caster to instantly traverse the Shadow Realm, disappearing from one location and reappearing at another within range. The transition is nearly instantaneous, making the spell invaluable for repositioning, evasion, and surprise attacks.

---
## Effects

- Instantly teleports the caster by traveling through the Shadow Realm. 
- Maximum range scales with **Darkness Affinity**. 
- Cooldown decreases with **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **20 uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** 8 seconds.

## Notes

- The movement is true teleportation rather than high-speed travel. 
- The spell briefly passes through the Shadow Realm, allowing the caster to ignore physical obstacles between the starting and destination points, provided the destination is within range. 
- Frequently used for ambushes, evasive maneuvers, and rapidly closing the distance to an opponent.
