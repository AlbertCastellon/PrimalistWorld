# Explosive Rampart

## Description
Conjures a protective barrier of condensed earth-aspected aether around the caster. After a short duration, the barrier violently detonates, damaging nearby enemies while shielding the owner until the explosion.

---
## Effects

- Summons a protective barrier around the caster for **30 seconds**. 
- The barrier automatically explodes when its duration ends. 
- Barrier durability scales with **Arcane Power**, **Toughness**, **Earth Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Explosion damage scales with **Toughness**, **Earth Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **80 uniums (Iron)** or **140 uniums (Silver)**. 
- **Cooldown:** 5 minutes.

---
## Restrictions

- [[Guardian]]. 
- **120 Toughness**. 
- **80 Arcane Power**. 
- **Earth Affinity:** Peak-Iron or higher.

## Notes

- The barrier serves both as a defensive tool and a delayed area-of-effect attack. 
- The explosion occurs automatically when the barrier expires. 
- The spell becomes significantly stronger when advanced to its Silver-rank version.
