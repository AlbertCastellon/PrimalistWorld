# Danger Sense

## Description
Passively heightens the user's instincts, providing an intuitive warning whenever danger is nearby. Rather than revealing the exact source or nature of the threat, the spell conveys a sense of urgency whose clarity depends on the user's perception.

---
## Effects

- Automatically warns the user of nearby danger. 
- Detection range and accuracy scale with **Aether Sensing** and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **no uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** None.

## Notes

- The spell is passive and remains active at all times. 
- It does not identify the source of the danger, only its presence and relative urgency. 
- Powerful concealment abilities, illusions, or large differences in power may reduce the spell's effectiveness.
