# Quartz Armor

## Description
Encases the caster in a durable layer of stone and crystalline quartz, greatly reinforcing their defenses while preserving mobility.

---
## Effects

- Covers the caster in stone and quartz for **2 minutes**. 
- Increases **Toughness by 5%**. 
- Defensive strength scales with **Arcane Power**, **Earth Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **20 uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** 4 minutes.

## Notes

- The spell is self-targeted and cannot be cast on other creatures. 
- The stone armor is magically created and disappears once the spell ends. 
- Commonly used before entering melee combat or when expecting heavy incoming damage.
