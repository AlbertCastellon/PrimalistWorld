# Blizzard

## Description
**Blizzard** conjures a devastating storm of razor-sharp ice shards that sweeps across the battlefield in a chosen direction, shredding everything caught within its path. The spell sacrifices the caster's physical resilience for overwhelming destructive power, leaving them significantly more vulnerable once the blizzard subsides.

---
## Effects

- Summons a powerful blizzard of ice shards that travels in a chosen direction.
- Deals continuous Ice damage to all targets caught within the storm.
- Potency scales with **Arcane Power**, **Ice Affinity**, and **spell quality**.
- Reduces the caster's **Toughness** by **50** for **5 minutes** after the spell ends.
- Consumes **400 uniums**.
- Cooldown: **6 hours**, reduced by higher spell quality.

---
## Restrictions

- Elementalist
- 180 Arcane Power
- Ice Affinity Low-Silver or higher
