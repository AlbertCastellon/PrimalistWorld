# Deathcoil Venom

## Description
Channels a potent venom into the owner's stingers, allowing the next successful strikes to inflict a debilitating toxin that weakens the target's body for an extended period.

---
## Effects

- Coats the owner's stingers with venom for **30 seconds**. 
- Successfully striking a target inflicts a poison that decreases **Strength** and **Toughness by 10 + 5%** for **3 minutes**. 
- Debuff potency and duration scale with **Arcane Power**, **Poison Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **50 uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** 10 minutes.

---
## Restrictions

- Requires venomous stingers or a compatible natural venom-delivery organ.

---
## Known Users

- [[Colossus]]

---
## Notes

- The poison is only applied if the venom-coated stinger successfully hits a target before the enchantment expires. 
- Particularly effective against heavily armored or physically powerful opponents, as it weakens both their offensive and defensive capabilities.
