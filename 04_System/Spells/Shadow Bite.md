# Shadow Bite

## Description
Empowers the user's next bite with corrosive Darkness aether, causing the attack to inflict both physical damage and lingering corrosive effects upon impact.

---
## Effects

- Enhances the next bite with corrosive dark smoke. 
- Damage scales with **Arcane Power**, **Darkness Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **10 uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** None.

## Known Users

- [[Nightmare]] (formerly)

---
## Notes

- The spell only affects the user's next bite attack. 
- The corrosive smoke deals additional damage on contact and can continue to erode the target after the initial strike. 
- Particularly effective against heavily armored or durable opponents due to its corrosive properties.
