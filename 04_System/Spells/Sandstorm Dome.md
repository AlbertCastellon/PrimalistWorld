# Sandstorm Dome

## Description
Conjures a dome of violently swirling sand centered on the caster, forming a protective barrier that intercepts incoming projectiles and ranged attacks from every direction.

---

## Effects

- Creates a **15-foot-radius** sand dome centered on the caster for **30 seconds**. 
- Blocks both **physical** and **non-physical ranged attacks**. 
- The dome's resilience, radius, and duration scale with **Toughness**, **Arcane Power**, **Earth Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **120 uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** 5 minutes.

## Notes

- The barrier only protects against **ranged attacks** and does not stop enemies from entering or attacking in melee. 
- The caster remains at the center of the dome while it is active. 
- Particularly effective against spellcasters, archers, and other ranged opponents.
