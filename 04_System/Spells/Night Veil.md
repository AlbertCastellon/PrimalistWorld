# Night Veil

## Description
Conjures a cloud of darkness-infused smoke that absorbs ambient light and blankets an area in unnatural gloom. The veil severely impairs both vision and aether perception, making it an effective spell for concealment, ambushes, and disrupting enemy awareness.

---
## Effects

- Creates a cloud of darkness for **5 minutes**. 
- Reduces **vision** and **aether sensing** by **65%** within the affected area. 
- **Area of effect**, **duration**, and **effectiveness** scale with **Darkness Affinity** and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **60 uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** None.

---
## Known Users

- [[Seth]]

---

## Notes

- Unlike ordinary smoke or mist, the cloud absorbs surrounding light, making it significantly more difficult to see through. 
- Particularly effective when combined with stealth, illusion, or mobility spells.
