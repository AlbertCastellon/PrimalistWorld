# Twin Mirages

## Description
Summons two tangible shadow doppelgängers that fight alongside the caster. Though unable to use spells, these mirages faithfully replicate the owner's physical combat style, overwhelming opponents through coordinated attacks and deception.

---
## Effects

- Summons **two physical shadow doppelgängers**. 
- Each doppelgänger possesses: 
	- **70%** of the owner's **Strength**. 
	- **70%** of the owner's **Agility**. 
	- **40%** of the owner's **Toughness**. 
- Doppelgängers **cannot cast spells**. 
- Their attributes and duration scale with **Arcane Power**, **Darkness Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- **Duration:** 33 seconds. 
- Consumes **300 uniums**.
- **Cooldown:** 20 minutes.

## Notes

- The doppelgängers are tangible constructs rather than mere illusions, allowing them to physically attack, block, and interact with their surroundings. 
- Since they cannot cast spells, they rely entirely on physical combat. 
- Particularly effective when combined with illusion-based abilities, making it difficult for opponents to distinguish the real caster from the copies.
