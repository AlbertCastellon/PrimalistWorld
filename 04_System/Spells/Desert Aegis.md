# Desert Aegis

## Description
Creates a protective aura of Earth aether centered on the caster, reinforcing the bodies of nearby allies and significantly increasing their resilience while they remain within its area of effect.

---

## Effects

- Creates a **20-foot-radius** protective zone centered on the caster for **5 minutes**. 
- Grants all allied creatures within the area, including the caster, **bonus Toughness equal to 15 + 3% of the caster's total Toughness**. 
- Bonus Toughness, duration, and radius scale with **Earth Affinity** and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **80 uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** 30 minutes.

## Notes

- The spell's protective bonus is based on the **caster's Toughness**, not the recipient's. 
- Allies only benefit while they remain inside the affected area. 
- Particularly effective for protecting frontline combatants and reinforcing defensive positions.
