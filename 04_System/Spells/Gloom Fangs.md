# Gloom Fangs

## Description
Condenses corrosive darkness around the owner's fangs, allowing the next bite to inject destructive shadow energy into the target. Repeated bites to the same wound progressively intensify the corrosive effect.

---
## Effects

- Coats the owner's fangs with condensed corrosive darkness. 
- The next successful bite inflicts a corrosive effect on the target. 
- Consecutive bites on the same location progressively increase the corrosion's potency. 
- Potency scales with **Arcane Power**, **Darkness Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **16 uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** None.

## Notes

- The spell rewards repeatedly striking the same wound rather than creating new ones. 
- Particularly effective against heavily armored or durable opponents, as the corrosive effect becomes increasingly destructive with successive bites.
