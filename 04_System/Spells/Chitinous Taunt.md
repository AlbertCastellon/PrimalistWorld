# Chitinous Taunt

## Description
Emits a commanding roar infused with Earth aether that compels nearby enemies to focus their hostility on the caster, drawing their attention away from allies.

---
## Effects

- Forces up to **4 targets** within a **10-yard radius** to prioritize attacking the caster for **10 seconds**. 
- Maximum number of affected targets, duration, and taunt potency scale with **Arcane Power**, **Earth Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **60 uniums**.
- **Cooldown:** 1 minute.

## Notes

- The spell influences the target's hostility rather than directly controlling their actions. 
- Particularly effective for protecting vulnerable allies by redirecting enemy attention. 
- Creatures with exceptional mental resistance or overwhelming differences in power may partially resist the taunt's effects.
