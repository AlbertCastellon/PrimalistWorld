# Scorpion Charge

## Description
Launches the caster forward in a powerful burst of speed, turning its momentum into a devastating charge capable of smashing through enemies and disrupting their formation.

---
## Effects

- Propels the caster forward in a sudden burst of speed. 
- Knocks back and briefly stuns enemies upon impact. 
- Impact force and stun potency scale with **Strength**, **Earth Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **30 uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** 30 seconds.

## Notes

- The spell is primarily used to initiate combat, close distances, or break through enemy lines. 
- The knockback and stun only occur if the charge successfully connects with a target.
