# Chains of Darkness

## Description
Summons a shadowy manifestation of the caster that binds a target with living chains of darkness, restricting their movements while inflicting relentless agony.

---

## Effects

- Summons a shadow that binds a single target. 
- Inflicts excruciating pain while reducing **Agility by 25** for **45 seconds**. 
- Duration scales with **Darkness Affinity** and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **50 uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** 45 seconds.

## Notes

- The summoned shadow exists solely to restrain its target and cannot attack independently.
- The chains hinder mobility rather than completely immobilizing the target. 
- Particularly effective against agile opponents or enemies attempting to flee.
