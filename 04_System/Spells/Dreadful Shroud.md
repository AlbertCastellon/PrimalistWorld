# Dreadful Shroud

## Description
Projects an overwhelming aura of terror that crushes the resolve of nearby enemies. Those caught within its influence suffer a significant reduction to their combat capabilities, while weaker-willed foes may become completely paralyzed with fear.

---
## Effects

- Projects an aura of fear within a **20-yard radius**. 
- Reduces all attributes of enemies inside the area by **10%**. 
- Immobilizes affected targets for a duration determined by their **mental fortitude**. 
- Aura potency and radius scale with **Arcane Power**, **Darkness Affinity**, and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **40 uniums per minute** while active. 
- **Cooldown:** None.

## Notes

- The spell remains active as long as the caster continues supplying uniums. 
- The weakening effect only applies to enemies currently within the aura's radius. 
- The duration of the immobilization varies depending on the target's mental resistance, making strong-willed opponents less susceptible to its effects. 
- Evolves from **[[Mantle of Terror]]**, greatly increasing both its debilitating power and crowd-control capabilities.
