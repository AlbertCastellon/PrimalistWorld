# Fog Shroud

## Description
Creates a dense veil of water-infused fog that obscures the battlefield, greatly reducing visibility and disrupting aether perception. It is primarily used to conceal movements, escape pursuit, or create opportunities for ambushes.

---
## Effects

- Creates a fog-covered area for **5 minutes**. 
- Reduces both **vision** and **aether sensing** by **60%** within the affected area. 
- **Area of effect**, **duration**, and **effectiveness** scale with **[[Water]] Affinity** and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **80 uniums**. 
- **Cooldown:** None.

---
## Restrictions

- **Classes:** [[Elementalist]], [[Primalist]], or[[ Rogue]]. 
- Minimum **55 Arcane Power**.

---

## Notes

- Particularly effective when combined with stealth, illusion, or mobility spells. 
- Since it hinders both sight and [[Aether]] perception, it can disrupt conventional tracking methods as well as direct line-of-sight combat.
