# Lightning Protection

## Description
A passive spell that reinforces the owner's body with lightning-aspected power, increasing their resilience while enhancing the Arcane Power of all Lightning-affinity spells.

---
## Effects

- Increases **Toughness by 10/15/20/25%**, depending on the spell's rank.
- Enhances all **Lightning-affinity spells** by an additional **5/10/15/20% Arcane Power**, depending on the spell's rank.
- Forms a **core** within the owner's chest containing a fragment of **Lacion Serry's Path**.
- The core amplifies one of the owner's existing traits or aptitudes and gradually guides them toward **Lacion's Path: Unstoppable Lightning**.
- May cause **very slight personality changes** over time.
