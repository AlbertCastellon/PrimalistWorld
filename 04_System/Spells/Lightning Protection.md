# Lightning Protection

## Description
A passive spell that reinforces the owner's body with lightning-aspected power, increasing their resilience while enhancing the Arcane Power of all [[Lightning]]-affinity spells.

---
## Effects

| Tier     | Effects                                                                                               |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Iron     | Increases Toughness by 10%. Enhances all Lightning-affinity spells by an additional 5% Arcane Power.  |
| Silver   | Increases Toughness by 15%. Enhances all Lightning-affinity spells by an additional 10% Arcane Power. |
| Gold     | Increases Toughness by 20%. Enhances all Lightning-affinity spells by an additional 15% Arcane Power. |
| Platinum | Increases Toughness by 25%. Enhances all Lightning-affinity spells by an additional 20% Arcane Power. |
