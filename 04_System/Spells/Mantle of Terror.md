# Mantle of Terror

## Description
Projects a terrifying aura that envelops the surrounding area, instilling primal fear in nearby enemies. Those caught within its influence suffer weakened combat capabilities and may momentarily freeze in terror.

---
## Effects

- Projects an aura of fear within a **16-yard radius**. 
- Reduces all attributes of enemies inside the area by **3.5%**. 
- Has a chance to briefly immobilize affected targets. 
- Aura potency and radius scale with **Darkness Affinity** and **spell quality**. 
- Consumes **30 uniums per minute** while active. 
- **Cooldown:** None.

---
## Restrictions

- 80 Arcane Power
- Iron [[Darkness]] Affinity

---
## Known Users

-  [[Nightmare]] (formerly)

---
## Notes

- The spell remains active as long as the caster continues supplying uniums. 
- The weakening effect only applies to enemies currently within the aura's radius. 
- Particularly effective against groups, as it simultaneously debuffs multiple opponents while disrupting their actions through fear.
