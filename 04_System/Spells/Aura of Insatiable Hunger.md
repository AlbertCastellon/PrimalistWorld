# Aura of Insatiable Hunger

## Description
Absorbs ambient [[Aether]] to replenish the user's Well and share the absorbed Aether with others. It also disrupts the use of artifacts and Spell-Scrolls, hindering those who lack sufficient power to overcome its effects.

---
## Effects

- Ambient Aether Absorption
- Aether Sharing
- Artifact and Spell-Scroll Interference
