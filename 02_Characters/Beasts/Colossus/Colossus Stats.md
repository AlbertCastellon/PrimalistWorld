## 🌱 Seeds
### [[Shard of the Molten Heaven]] | [[Copper Tier|Copper]] ~ Epic |

#### Integrated Seed Effects

- Increases **Strength** by 6.
- Increases **Toughness** by 8%.
- Increases **Arcane Power** by 5%.
- Reduces the cooldown of Earth spells by 25%.

### [[Titan Gem Shard|Titan Gem Shard]] | [[Iron Tier|Iron]] ~ Epic |

#### Integrated Seed Effects

- Increases potency of all [[Earth]]-affinity spells by 7% Toughness.
- Boosts **Toughness** by 12%.
- Grants 60% resistance to immobilizing, stunning, or slowing effects.

### [[Scale of the Emerald Leviathan]] | [[Iron Tier|Iron]] ~ Legendary |

#### Integrated Seed Effects

- Increases **Toughness** by 25%.
- Increases **Arcane Power** by 5%.
- Increases **Agility** by an amount equal to 10% of the owner's total **Toughness**, including all bonuses granted by equipment, spells, and other enhancements.
