## Appearance  
Nightmare is a black wolf the size of a horse, he has red eyes, and sharp fangs.

## Personality
Nightmare is fiercely competitive, prideful, and highly driven to become stronger. He was once prone to jealousy whenever [[Seth]] or [[Colossus]] achieved something beyond his own capabilities, always striving to remain the strongest member of the pack. As he matured, that pride shifted from personal ambition to pride in the pack itself. Although he still dislikes seeing Seth copy his spells, he now appreciates the growth of his companions instead of feeling threatened by it. Despite his aloof and stubborn demeanor, Nightmare is deeply loyal and will go to great lengths to protect those he recognizes as his pack.

## Abilities  

- High Agility and damage capacity
- Capable to create really useful spells in crisis.

## History  
Nightmare was rescued by [[Seth]] while he was still a pup. Orphaned after his mother died, he was being chased by several [[Arcane Beasts]] that Seth ultimately killed. When Seth collapsed from his injuries after the battle, Nightmare instinctively remained by his side, guarding him until he awoke.

From that day onward, Seth raised him, feeding him [[Beaststones]] and hunting alongside him. Their bond deepened when Nightmare was nearly killed by a powerful poison. Desperate to save him, Seth used **Link** to share his [[Aether]], allowing Nightmare to survive. The experience permanently strengthened their bond, granting them the ability to communicate mentally while also accelerating Nightmare's intellectual development.

Nightmare eventually surpassed Seth in Rank, something he takes great pride in. Ever since, he has been determined to maintain that advantage, refusing to let Seth overtake him.
