## Overview  
Vorath **the Great**
## Appearance  
A colossal wyvern with a massive, scarred body covered in hardened scales. Two obsidian horns protrude from its crocodilian skull, while thick, leathery wings stretch wide enough to cast a shadow over an entire platoon. Its powerful talons dig deep into the ground, which freezes beneath its feet. Each breath releases plumes of icy mist that drain warmth from the surrounding air.
