# Mael

## Overview

## Appearance

**Mael** is a handsome young man with long blond hair usually tied into a ponytail that reaches the middle of his back and striking blue eyes. His face is marked by sharp features and a naturally charming smile, while his lean, athletic build gives him the appearance of someone born for agility rather than hard labor.

---
## Personality

He is easy-going.

---
## History

Inhabitant of Sunatown, and [[Seth]]'s friend, his dream is to become a powerful Wielder. He is often teased because of his flirtatious tendencies.
