### Will of the Defiers
Allows to improve the Wielder spells' quality faster, and evolve them beyond their normal limits.
It can also alter the Wielder's [[Path]].

### Alpha's Resonance
Allows the Wielder to benefit from the seeds their contracted beasts absorb, getting a fourth part of the bonuses the seeds give.
