## 🧬 Talents

### Will of the Defiers
Allows to improve the Wielder spells' quality faster, and evolve them beyond their normal limits.
It can also alter the Wielder's [[Path]].

### Alpha's Resonance
Allows the Wielder to benefit from the seeds their contracted beasts absorb, getting a fourth part of the bonuses the seeds give.

## 🌱 Seeds (Integrated Effects)  

### [[Claw of the Primal Tiger]] | [[Copper Tier|Copper]] ~ Legendary+ |

- Increases **Agility** by 7%.
- Increases **Strength** by 8%.
- Reduces the cooldown of Rage-type spells by 85% and boosts their effects by 25%, but amplifies the aftermath penalties by 20%.

### [[Petrified Tree Sap|Petrified Tree Sap]] | [[Iron Tier|Iron]] ~ ??? |
  
- Reduces damage from elemental-affinity spells by 25%.  
- Increases potency of all [[Null|Null]] spells by 20%.  
- Boosts **Regeneration** by 30%.  

### Alpha's Resonance
#### [[Nightmare]]
  
- Boosts **Arcane Power** by 7.5%.
- Boosts **Agility** by 7.5%.
- Boosts **Strength** by 1.25%.
- Boosts **Toughness** by 2.
- Reduces the cooldown of Dark spells by 7%.
- Increases the potency of all Darkness-affinity spells by 5% **Arcane Power**.
- Grant a 7.5% chance of resetting the cooldown of any Dark spell on cast.
#### [[Colossus]]
  
- Boosts **Strength** by 2.
- Boosts **Toughness** by 21.25%.
- Increases **Arcane Power** by 2.5%.
- Reduces the cooldown of Earth spells by 6.25%.
- Increases the potency of all Earth-affinity spells by 2% Toughness.
- Grants 15% resistance to immobilizing, stunning, or slowing effects.
- Increases **Agility** by an amount equal to 2.5% of the owner's total Toughness, including all bonuses granted by equipment, spells, and other enhancements.
