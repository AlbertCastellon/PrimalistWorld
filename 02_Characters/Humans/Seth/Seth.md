## Personality
Seth is a noble and loyal person, shaped by the years he spent in a poor village caring for his sick mother. The support he received from his neighbors taught him the value of solidarity and looking after others, while the immense effort required to afford his mother's treatment instilled in him a strong sense of perseverance and determination.

As a [[Primalist]], Seth was forced to repeatedly put his life at risk in order to grow stronger. This made him somewhat reckless, as he is willing to push himself into dangerous situations when the potential reward is worth the risk. However, his recklessness is rarely motivated by simple carelessness. Seth usually tries to choose situations where he believes he can succeed, constantly walking the line between avoiding unnecessary risks and pursuing opportunities that could lead to significant growth. Despite this, his willingness to take risks can sometimes leave him in situations far more dangerous than he anticipated.

Seth has a strong sense of justice and little tolerance for cruelty or injustice. He is willing to risk his life to protect even complete strangers when he believes it is the right thing to do. Once he has decided that something is right, he is exceptionally difficult to dissuade, even when doing so puts him in danger. His determination can border on stubbornness, and he would rather face death than submit to someone he does not respect or believe deserves his obedience.

Despite his reckless nature, Seth is not driven solely by ambition. His experiences have made him deeply appreciative of the people who stand by him, and he feels a strong responsibility toward those he considers his own. This combination of compassion, determination, and an unwillingness to compromise his principles defines much of his character.
